---
title: Connect Companion
description: Use the deck in Bitfocus Companion over Wi-Fi or Ethernet, as a Satellite surface or as a Network Dock.
---

[Bitfocus Companion](https://bitfocus.io/companion) can use the deck in two ways.

| | Satellite | Network Dock |
|---|---|---|
| Who connects | The board connects to Companion | Companion connects to the board |
| Companion must find the board | No, so Docker without host networking works | Yes, by discovery or its address |

Satellite is the better choice. When Companion connects as a dock and its Satellite answers on the same address, the board moves it to Satellite by itself.

## Satellite

<Steps>
<Step>
In Companion, keep **Settings › Satellite** on. It needs Satellite API 1.9 or newer, tested with Companion 5.2.
</Step>
<Step>
In the web UI, open **Sources** ([demo](/demo/#sources)) and enter the Companion address. Every Companion that announces itself in the network is listed too. One click connects it.
</Step>
<Step>
The deck shows up under **Surfaces** as `streamdeck:<serial>`, the same id as over USB. Your pages and surface settings stay.
</Step>
</Steps>

| Address | Goes to |
|---|---|
| `192.168.1.10` | TCP port 16622 |
| `192.168.1.10:17000` | TCP on that port |
| `ws://192.168.1.10:16623` | Companion's WebSocket port |
| `wss://companion.example.org/satellite` | A TLS reverse proxy in front of port 16623 |

A `wss://` address needs a certificate from a public CA. For any other certificate, paste its SHA-256 as the pin. With an internal CA that renews often, pin the CA.

```bash
openssl x509 -noout -fingerprint -sha256 -in cert.pem
```

### Layout

Companion sees two rows of keys, the touch strip as four 200 × 100 zones, then the four dials, the same as its own Stream Deck plugin. A tap presses the zone, a sideways swipe changes the page, a swipe up or down turns that zone like a dial.

## Network Dock

Companion finds the board under **Surfaces › Discover**, or you add its address as a remote surface.

To stop the move to Satellite, remove the Companion address or answer **Nein** (no) on the deck. A new address turns it on again.

<Callout title="Behind a proxy">
The board then sees only the proxy's address and cannot end the dock connection. Companion shows the deck twice, the second as `streamdeck:<serial>-dev2`.
</Callout>

## Limits

- One Companion over Satellite at a time. For more programs see [Multiple hosts](/docs/hosts).
- On a Stream Deck Neo only the eight keys.
