Deck Connector is free firmware for a small board. The connector drives an [Elgato Stream Deck](/docs/hardware#decks) over USB and puts it on your network. It works like an [Elgato Network Dock](https://www.elgato.com/us/en/p/network-dock-stream-deck), and adds Wi-Fi.

<Topology />

- [Companion](/docs/companion), the [Elgato app](/docs/elgato-app) and [your own server](/docs/own-server) connect at once. You [pick the source](/docs/hosts) that draws.
- [Home Assistant](/docs/home-assistant) and [Matter](/docs/matter) can get every key press too. Both are optional.
- Wi-Fi and Ethernet at the same time. [When the cable fails](/docs/network), Wi-Fi takes over.
- [Signed updates](/docs/updates). A firmware that does not start rolls back by itself.

## What you need

- **Board.** A [Waveshare ESP32-P4-WIFI6-POE-ETH](https://www.waveshare.com/esp32-p4-wifi6-poe-eth.htm), with chip revision v3.1 or later. The flasher checks it. See [Hardware](/docs/hardware).
- **Deck.** A Stream Deck + (tested). [Other models](/docs/hardware#decks) should work too.
- **Power.** A USB-C power supply, or a PoE switch port.
- **Computer.** Chrome or Edge, to [flash](/flash/) once.

## Try it first

The [demo](/demo/) runs the real web UI with a virtual deck in your browser. Nothing to install.
