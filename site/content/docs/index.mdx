title: Introduction
description: DeckConnector puts an Elgato Stream Deck on your network.

DeckConnector is firmware for a small PoE board. The board drives an Elgato Stream Deck over USB and puts it on the network.
Bitfocus Companion, the Elgato App and your own programs then use the deck as if it were plugged into the computer.

- Companion connects over its Satellite API, or sees the board as an Elgato Network Dock.
- The Elgato App sees the board as a Network Dock.
- Your own server draws the keys over WebSocket and gets every press, turn and touch.
- Several programs can be connected at once. You pick the one that draws, on the deck or in the web UI.
- LAN and Wi-Fi at the same time. When the LAN fails, the board keeps going over Wi-Fi.
- Signed updates. A new firmware that does not start rolls back by itself.

## What you need

- A [Waveshare ESP32-P4-WIFI6-POE-ETH](/docs/hardware) board.
- A Stream Deck +. Other models are in the driver but not tested yet, see [Hardware](/docs/hardware).
- A PoE switch port, or a USB-C power supply.
- A computer with Chrome or Edge to flash the firmware.

## Try it first

The [demo](/demo/) runs the real web UI with a virtual deck in your browser. Nothing is installed.
