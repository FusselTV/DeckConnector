---
title: Elgato App
description: The Elgato Stream Deck app uses the board as a Network Dock.
---

The board answers like an Elgato Network Dock with the deck plugged into it. The Elgato App finds it in the network by itself.

<Steps>
<Step>

Open the Elgato App on a computer in the same network as the board.

</Step>
<Step>

If the deck does not show up, add it in the app under **Settings › Devices › Add network deck** with the board's address.

</Step>
</Steps>

A board without a deck does not announce the dock. The app ignores a dock without a deck until the app restarts.

## Pairing

The board lists every computer that connected, by its MAC address, up to 8. A laptop with a new address from DHCP stays the same host.

With **Paired hosts only** on, the first computer still gets in by itself. The board turns away every later one until you accept it on the deck or in the web UI, or until you open pairing for 5 minutes. A denied app asks again only after it restarts.
