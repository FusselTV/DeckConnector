<SetupFlow />

1. **Flash.** Connect the board's USB-C port to your computer and open the [flasher](/flash/). It can also join your Wi-Fi. Problems? See [Flashing problems](/docs/flash-troubleshooting).
2. **Plug in.** The deck goes into the USB-A port. Power the connector with a USB-C power supply, or with a network cable from a PoE port. The deck then shows its address.
3. **Set up.** Open the address in a browser and follow the wizard. See [First setup](/docs/first-setup).
4. **Connect.** [Companion](/docs/companion), the [Elgato app](/docs/elgato-app) or your [own server](/docs/own-server). The first one that connects takes the deck.

Using [Home Assistant](/docs/home-assistant)? It finds the connector by itself and gets every key press. It works next to Companion or the Elgato app. For Apple Home or Google Home, install the [Matter firmware](/docs/matter).

## The deck menu

While a source draws, hold the first and the last dial for 2 seconds. On a deck without dials, hold the first and the last key. The menu closes after a minute without input.

<DeckMenu />

The deck speaks the language you set up with. The top left key of the menu switches between English and German.
