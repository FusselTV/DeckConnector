---
title: Benchmarks
description: Measured speed of DeckConnector next to the Elgato Network Dock, on the same Stream Deck +.
---

On the deck, DeckConnector is as fast as the [Elgato Network Dock](https://www.elgato.com/us/en/p/network-dock-stream-deck) or faster. Here are the numbers.

Both devices drove the same Stream Deck + with the same Companion. Values are medians. A camera frame is 17 ms, so differences under 5 to 10 ms are noise.

## Companion

<BenchBars title="Time until the deck shows it, lower is better" unit="ms" rows={[
  ['Companion variable to key', 15, 29],
  ['Companion page change, 7 keys', 45, 49],
  ['Full page, 8 keys and strip, without Companion', 31, 49],
]} />

<BenchBars title="Seconds, lower is better" unit="s" rows={[
  ['Companion restart to home page', 1.2, 1.96],
  ['Network gone, deck leaves the dead source', 5.4, 6.5],
]} />

Companion numbers are for both devices in Network Dock mode. Companion sends both the same bytes, about 26 KB/s on one page change per second.

## Elgato app

<BenchBars title="Seconds, lower is better" unit="s" rows={[
  ['App start to deck connected', 1.96, 2.62],
  ['Page change in the editor', 0.13, 0.12],
]} />

| Test | DeckConnector | Network Dock |
|---|---|---|
| 8 animated keys | 252 frames/s | about 200 frames/s |
| Picture confirmed after its last part | 0.8 ms | 4.6 ms |

## Power cut

| Test | DeckConnector | Network Dock |
|---|---|---|
| PoE power cut to Companion picture, Network Dock mode | 7.9 s | 17.6 s |
| PoE power cut to Companion picture, Satellite | 6.75 s | No Satellite |
| PoE power cut, Elgato app connected again | 9 to 13.5 s, in 3 of 4 tries | Not within 4 minutes (0 of 2) |

## Own server protocol

One key, from the image to the confirmation after the USB transfer, takes 1.2 to 1.6 ms. The Network Dock has no such protocol.

## Where the dock is better

| Test | DeckConnector | Network Dock |
|---|---|---|
| TCP receive window | 23 KB | 36 to 61 KB |
| mDNS answer to a PTR query | 118 ms | about 1 ms |

Neither is visible on the deck. A page arrives in 2.8 ms on both.
